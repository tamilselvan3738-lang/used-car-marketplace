Our Story & Our Growth Journey

Founded in 2018 by automotive engineers and software architects, AutoNexus began as a solution to a broken industry. Today, we power transparent car transactions across 48 states.

Built by car enthusiasts tired of the traditional dealership runaround.

In 2018, buying a used car required either visiting a dealership with opaque markups or meeting unknown private sellers in parking lots. We envisioned a third path: an algorithmic platform where every vehicle is mechanically audited, legally guaranteed, and delivered right to your driveway.

By building proprietary computer-vision diagnostic scanners and partnering with nationwide ASE-certified inspection centers, we transformed pre-owned car shopping into a frictionless, 100% digital experience.
